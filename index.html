My story

Starting with the data

At Lucid Software, after starting out at Accenture, I saw that getting data meant waiting on someone who knew where it lived. I built the fix: a dbt project that modeled our data in Snowflake, orchestrated with Airflow, with self-serve UI on top. It became the single source of truth for metric definitions, used by ~100 people, and cut the time to retrieve data by ~40%. It was my first real product.

Moving closer to the decisions

Building data tools was a great start, but I wanted to be closer to business decisions. So I moved into product data science at MakeSpace and Loom, where I built activation metrics to lift 28-day engagement, designed and ran A/B tests, and worked closely with executives to shape product decisions based on data.

Where I am now

I build AI-powered data tools hands-on, and drive what gets built next, so leadership can move the business faster. I focus on making them scalable and reusable, from tools to Claude Skills, so one solution serves many teams.

The thread hasn't changed: understand how the numbers are made, then use them to decide what data products to build.
