My story

Starting with the data

At Lucid Software, getting data meant waiting on whoever knew where it lived. I built a dbt project on Snowflake and Airflow with a self-serve UI on top. It became the single source of truth for metrics, used by ~100 people, and cut the time to retrieve data by ~40%. It was my first real product.

Moving closer to the decisions

I wanted to be closer to business decisions, so I moved into product data science at MakeSpace and Loom. I built activation metrics to lift 28-day engagement, ran A/B tests, and partnered with executives to shape product decisions.

Where I am now

At Salesforce, I build AI-powered data tools hands-on and drive what gets built next, so leadership can move faster. I make them reusable, from tools to Claude Skills, so one solution serves many teams.

The thread hasn't changed: understand how the numbers are made, then use them to decide what data products to build.
